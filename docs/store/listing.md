## English

**Name:** `{{NAME}}`
**Subtitle (30):** Outfits from your own closet
**Promotional text (170):** Photograph your clothes once. Every morning: an outfit from what you already own, for today's weather — and it learns what you actually wear.

**Keywords (100, comma-separated, no spaces):**
`outfit,wardrobe,closet,what to wear,style,clothes,weather,capsule,planner,ootd,dress,organizer`

**Description:**

> Open the app, see what to wear. {{NAME}} builds outfits from the clothes you already own — not from a shop.
>
> **Add a piece in seconds.** Photograph it; category, colours, pattern, warmth and season fill themselves in. Fix anything, save, done.
>
> **Dressed for today.** Outfits for the weather where you are and for where you're going — everyday, work, a date, a formal evening, sport, travel. Say it in your own words ("lots of walking, want to look sharp") and the suggestion follows.
>
> **It learns your taste.** Like, skip or mark what you wore; the next outfits lean towards what you actually choose. A short style summary grows from your own wardrobe, not from a quiz.
>
> **See what's missing.** Which pieces unlock the most outfits, which occasions you can't dress for yet, what to add next.
>
> **Your own outfits, too.** Build and edit combinations by hand; keep the ones you love.
>
> **Private by design.** Your photos stay private, nothing is sold or used to train models, and you can download or delete everything from your profile at any time.
>
> Try it with a sample wardrobe first — no account needed. Free plan: 50 pieces and 5 suggestions a day.

**What's new (1.0.0):** First release.
